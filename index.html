Seron Darkness

The Dark Star · Hero of the Zavila Galaxy · Leader of the Guardians
Role
Central Protagonist · Architect of the Rise Of Seron Universe
Alignment
Protective · Relentless · Shadow-forged Light
Lore overview

Seron Darkness is the living convergence of shadow and starlight—an entity born at the edge of a collapsing nebula where the Zavila Galaxy first learned what true danger meant. Known across systems as The Dark Star, Seron carries a gravity of presence that bends both light and destiny toward a single purpose: protection of the innocent and preservation of cosmic balance.

As The Hero of the Zavila Galaxy, Seron rose from obscurity, wielding power that most beings could barely comprehend. Instead of ruling through fear, he chose guardianship—standing between fragile worlds and the forces that would consume them.

Seron is also the leader of the guardians, a constellation of warriors, mystics, and sentient constructs bound by oath to defend the galaxy.

Beyond his deeds, Seron is the creator of the Rise Of Seron Universe itself—both in narrative and metaphysical terms.

Kole Rogue

The Arsonist · Fireborne Clone of Seron · Wielder of the Fire Axe
Role
Active Guardian · Fire Combat Specialist
Alignment
Chaotic Good · Volatile · Fiercely Loyal
Lore overview

Kole Rogue is one of Seron’s clones—born from the same cosmic blueprint but shaped by flame instead of shadow. Where Seron is calm and gravitational, Kole is explosive, unpredictable, and driven by a burning instinct that refuses to be contained.

Known as The Arsonist, Kole wields a standard fire axe—nothing mystical, nothing forged from star-metal. Yet in his hands, it becomes a weapon of terrifying precision, amplified by his innate fire abilities.

As an active guardian, Kole is deployed on missions requiring destructive force, rapid response, and overwhelming intimidation. He is the fire Seron unleashes when shadows alone aren’t enough.

Abilities

  • Fire-augmented melee combat.
  • Extreme resistance to heat and combustion.
  • Explosive bursts of speed during combat.
Equipment
  • Standard fire axe (non-magical).
  • Heat-resistant guardian armor.
  • Flame-channeling gauntlets.

Michael Rogue

The Pocket Watch User · Temporal Clone of Seron
Role
Active Guardian · Temporal Operations Specialist
Artifact
Pocket Watch crafted by Grayson Talon
Lore overview

Michael Rogue is a clone of Seron whose abilities revolve around a single artifact: a pocket watch engineered by Grayson Talon, one of the most brilliant technomancers in the Rise Of Seron Universe.

The watch grants Michael subtle temporal manipulation—brief rewinds, momentary freezes, and micro-adjustments that allow him to survive situations no normal guardian could.

As an active guardian, Michael is deployed for precision missions, time-sensitive operations, and scenarios where even a single second can determine the fate of an entire system.

  • Momentary time freezes for precision actions.

  • Instinctive danger premonition.
Equipment
  • Pocket watch crafted by Grayson Talon.
  • Guardian field attire.
  • Temporal stabilizer band.

Andrew OverDark

The Time Breaker God · The Black Rose · Survivor of the Angelic Purges
Role
Time Breaker God · Former Zavara Engineer
Homeworld
OverDarkis
Lore overview

Andrew OverDark was once a brilliant engineer from Zavara, known for designing temporal stabilizers and chronal engines. His life changed forever during the Angelic Purges, when his wife, Lydia Lasil, was murdered — an event that shattered his mortality and ignited the birth of the Time Breaker God.

His ascension transformed him into a towering, reality-fracturing deity whose presence bends timelines like soft metal. The grief he carries is woven into his power, giving him the ability to break, rewind, fracture, or collapse time itself.

Andrew now resides on OverDarkis, a world marked by a dark green sky, blood moon, and fields of Black Roses — the symbol of his rebirth and the legacy of his fallen wife.

Casey Vigor

The Isotope Hero · The Last Vigor · Rage-Forged Guardian
Role
Isotope Warrior · Guardian Combat Specialist
Alignment
Angry · Determined · Unbreakable
Lore overview

Casey Vigor is the final surviving member of the Vigor bloodline — a family wiped out entirely during the purges. The trauma of losing everyone he ever knew forged him into a relentless, rage-driven hero whose power is fueled by Isotope energy.

His isotope abilities allow him to amplify strength, speed, durability, and energy output far beyond normal guardian limits. When enraged, his isotope core becomes unstable, granting him massive boosts in power at the cost of physical strain.

Despite his anger, Casey fights to protect others so no one else suffers the fate his bloodline did. His fury is not mindless — it is focused, weaponized, and aimed at preventing future purges.

Profile data
Abilities
  • Isotope energy amplification.
  • Massive strength and speed boosts.
  • Unstable rage mode with red isotope output.
  • Energy projection and isotope shielding.
Traits
  • Usually angry due to bloodline loss.
  • Protective of younger guardians.
  • Unwilling to let history repeat itself.
  • Has contingency forms when pushed to the brink.

HawkBat Rogue

The Scarlet Elemental · Scarlet Clone of Seron · Winged Combatant
Role
Active Guardian · Elemental Combat Specialist
Alignment
Chaotic Neutral · Fierce · Unpredictable
Lore overview

HawkBat Rogue is one of Seron’s clones — but unlike the others, his power manifested as pure scarlet elemental energy. His body generates volatile red aura waves that enhance strength, speed, and aerial maneuverability.

Born from Seron’s genetic blueprint but altered by cosmic instability, HawkBat developed wing-like energy structures that allow him to glide, dive, and strike with terrifying precision. His scarlet aura burns hot enough to melt reinforced guardian armor.

HawkBat is unpredictable, fierce, and often difficult for other guardians to work with. Despite this, he is loyal to Seron and the guardian order — even if his methods are chaotic and explosive.

Traits

Ral Awakening

The Crimson Swordsman · Wielder of The Crimson Fear · Fear Manipulator
Origin
Varkuun Loyalty
Alignment
Stoic · Controlled · Fear-Born
Lore overview

Ral Awakening is the silent terror of the guardian order — a stoic warrior forged from trauma, discipline, and fear itself. His sword, The Crimson Fear, originates from Varkuun Loyalty, a world known for its brutal bounty hunters and smuggling rings.

As a child, Ral endured severe abuse from his parents. In a moment of uncontrollable awakening, his fear manipulation surged, causing him to kill them — an event that shaped his cold, emotionless demeanor.

Ral’s mastery of fear allows him to weaponize terror, suppress enemy morale, and manipulate emotional states. He is quiet, observant, and deadly — a guardian whose presence alone can end a battle.

Samuel Andreas

Wave · The Power Copier · Adaptive Guardian
Role
Copy Specialist · Guardian Support Combatant
Alignment
Calm · Analytical · Adaptive
Lore overview

Samuel Andreas, known as Wave, is one of the most versatile guardians in the Rise Of Seron Universe. His ability allows him to copy powers, weapons, and abilities from other guardians simply by observing or making brief contact.

Wave’s power does not steal or drain — it mirrors. He can replicate nearly any ability, though only at partial strength unless he trains with it. This makes him invaluable in missions requiring adaptability, counter-strategy, or rapid response.

Samuel’s calm demeanor contrasts with the chaos of battle. He analyzes, adapts, and shifts forms faster than most enemies can react.

Profile data
Abilities
  • Copies powers, weapons, and abilities from allies.
  • Can combine multiple copied abilities temporarily.
  • Enhanced reflexes during ability shifts.
  • Strategic combat analysis.
Traits
  • Calm under pressure.
  • Highly intelligent and observant.
  • Acts as a tactical support guardian.
  • Rarely shows emotion during missions.

Samuel Stardust

The Stardust Vampire · Vexx-Created · Spectral Summoner
Role
Spectral Summoner · Guardian Combat Specialist
Origin
Created by The Vexx Empire
Lore overview

Samuel Stardust is not a traditional vampire — he is a bio-engineered creation of the Vexx Empire, designed as a living weapon. His body contains stardust-infused blood, granting him supernatural strength, regeneration, and spectral summoning.

Alex Chronos freed Samuel from Vexx control, restoring his autonomy and giving him a place among the guardians. Samuel now uses his spectral abilities to fight alongside allies.

His summoned spectrals are star-like entities capable of independent combat, shielding, and coordinated attacks.

  • Night vision and cosmic perception.
Traits

  • Grateful to Alex Chronos for his freedom.

  • Prefers fighting alongside spectrals.

Alex Chronos

The Chronos Sniper · Time Sniper · Memory-Restored Guardian
Role
Temporal Sniper · Guardian Marksman
Origin
Zavara
Lore overview

Alex Chronos was born in Zavara, where he lived with his brother until the Vexx Empire attacked their city. During the invasion, Alex was separated from his brother and captured. The Vexx Empire wiped his memories, turning him into a living weapon.

Seron eventually found Alex and restored his memories, freeing him from Vexx control. With his identity returned, Alex became one of the most precise guardians in the order.

As the Chronos Sniper, Alex uses time manipulation to slow, freeze, or accelerate moments during combat, allowing him to land impossible shots.

  • Enhanced reflexes during chronal focus.
Traits

  • Grateful to Seron for restoring his memories.
  • Protective of Samuel Stardust.
